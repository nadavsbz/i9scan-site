Privacy

Last updated September 15, 2026

I9Scan is in early access. This page covers this website: the fine-exposure checker and the early-access form. It will be updated before the upload service opens.

What we collect

  • What you type into the early-access form: your work email, number of locations, the format of your I-9 records, whether you use E-Verify, the plan you chose and any note you add.
  • Your fine-check answers, if you submit the form: number of forms, estimated error share, industry, prior-violation answer and whether you have received a Notice of Inspection. The checker itself runs in your browser and sends nothing unless you submit the form.
  • How you arrived: the campaign or referral tag in the page address (for example utm_source), if there is one.

We do not ask for, and this site never receives, Forms I-9, employee names, Social Security numbers or identity documents.

How we use it

  • To send you one email with your upload link when your account is ready.
  • To understand which businesses need the service and which plan they prefer.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Who processes it

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Formspree receives early-access form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces this page uses.
  • Google Ads may record that a visit came from an ad, if you arrived through one.

How long we keep it

Early-access details are kept until the service launches and for 12 months after, then deleted, unless you become a customer.

Your choices

To have your details deleted, submit the early-access form again with the same email and write "delete my data" in the note. We will delete them and not contact you again.

Not legal advice

I9Scan is software, not a law firm, and is not affiliated with USCIS, ICE or DHS.
